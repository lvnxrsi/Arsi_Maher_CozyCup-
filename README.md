# Arsi_Maher_CozyCup-

**Description**
Welcome to Cozy Cup: Cutesy Edition!
Our website is designed with soft pastel colors to create a vibrant yet calming vibe that perfectly matches our menu. We offer sweet and adorable treats that are sure to tickle your taste buds!

For the design, we used HTML, CSS, and a mix of creative elements from Canva and other online resources to bring everything together.


**Functionality**
Our website may have minimal functions, but each one makes a big impact! For example, when you click buttons, they change colors, making it easier for visitors to navigate and find exactly what they’re looking for.
We also added navigation buttons on the upper right corner of the website to help users jump directly to certain sections, such as the contact information, menu, and more.
On our menu page, photos zoom in when you hover your mouse over them. This feature helps customers see details more clearly, ensuring they won’t get confused when ordering. We achieved this effect by placing the photos in HTML and styling them with CSS.
Lastly, we added an information section and a message wall where customers can leave feedback, inquiries, and other concerns, helping us connect better with everyone who visits Cozy Cup.
